# Sentinel's Journal

## 2026-10-28 - Formalization of Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure Policy
**Vulnerability:** Absence of a standard security disclosure policy (`SECURITY.md`) in a security-focused, public architecture repository holding STRIDE threat models and audit documentation.
**Learning:** The project is in pre-implementation / HOLD state where incident response procedures were planned conceptually in `docs/02-diseno/security/incident-response.md` (§Pendientes), but lacked an operational entrypoint directing researchers to private reporting channels rather than public issues.
**Prevention:** Establish a project-level `SECURITY.md` defining private reporting mechanisms, contact points with co-owners, and SLA commitments.
